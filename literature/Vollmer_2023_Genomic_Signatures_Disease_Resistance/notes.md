# Genomic Signatures of Disease Resistance in Endangered Staghorn Corals
## Citation: 
Steven V. Vollmer et al. ,Genomic signatures of disease resistance in endangered staghorn corals.Science381,1451-1454(2023).DOI:10.1126/science.adi3601
## What Was the biological question?
How does genetic variation among staghorn corals influence their susceptibility or resistance to white band disease?
## What organism was studied?
Staghorn coral
## What genomic data were used?
The researchers used whole-genome resequencing data from 76 staghorn coral genotypes collected from Florida and Panama. They analyzed genetic variation, particularly single-nucleotide polymorphisms, to identify genomic regions associated with resistance to white band disease.
## What was the major finding?
The researchers found that resistance to white band disease in staghorn coral is influenced by multiple regions across the genome. They identified 10 genomic regions and 73 SNPs associated with disease resistance, and showed that polygenic scores could be used to predict which corals are more resistant.
## How could the result matter for coral conservation?
This matters because identifying genetic markers associated with disease resistance could help conservationists select and breed disease-resistant staghorn corals for restoration. This may improve the survival of restored coral populations and help preserve genetic diversity as coral diseases continue to threaten reefs.
